I BUILD SCALABLE, INTELLIGENT AND RELIABLE SOFTWARE.

I'm ANUSH, a Full Stack Developer & GenAI Engineer at Hexaware Technologies, working on backend systems since October 2024. My focus is system design — how services divide responsibility and stay correct under concurrent load.

Making something work once is easy; making it hold when everything arrives at the same time and half of it fails is the part I care about. That shows up as Spring Boot microservices, distributed locking, message queues, and circuit breakers built to survive real failure, not just the happy path.

Lately that means designing with LLMs in the loop — RAG, LangChain, agents that call real tools — where the hard part is never the model, it's everything around it. My direction is backend engineering that treats AI as a component to integrate safely, not a shortcut around good architecture.
